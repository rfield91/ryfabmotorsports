Improve cooling, downforce, and drag. These are designed to fit the RX8 hood, but they should fit well on many different cars.

## What's included

- A pair of un-bent hood vents
- 3/16" Rivets

## Installation Tips

While they are bendable by hand, I recommend using something like these [panel seamers](https://www.harborfreight.com/6-inch-jaw-straight-sheet-metal-seamer-98728.html) for best results.

Run a piece of string across the hood to use as a straight end to align the leading edges. Place one side where you like the position and mark the location of one of the holes in the leading edge. Measure the distance to the edge of the hood and replicate that mark for the opposite side.
