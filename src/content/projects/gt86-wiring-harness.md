The car was stripped to a shell for a road-race build with a standalone ECU. The factory harness
was cut out entirely rather than modified.

## What we're doing

- Laying out the full harness on the bench against a drawn circuit list before it goes in the car.
- Building a fuse and relay panel, dash switchgear, and a master kill switch to series rules.
- Labelling every circuit at both ends.
- Handing over a wiring diagram with the car so anyone can service it later, not just us.
