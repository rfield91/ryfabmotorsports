This car came in needing a custom downpipe and exhaust after they swapped in a K24Z2. The single-port head of the K24Z2 made fitting a 3" downpipe relatively simple. A cast elbow was scavenged from an off the shelf downpipe that fit a different platform. The owner plans to run this car with ChampCar in 2027.
