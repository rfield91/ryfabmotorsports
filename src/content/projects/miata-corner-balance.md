Regular autocross car. The owner supplied the coilovers; the job was installation, balance and
alignment, not selection.

## What we did

- Installed the coilovers and swaybars, checked for clearance through full travel.
- Corner balanced on the scales with the driver in the seat and a full tank, per the class rules
  for the car's event.
- Aligned to a printed autocross setup sheet: more front camber than a street alignment, toe set
  for turn-in over straight-line wear.
