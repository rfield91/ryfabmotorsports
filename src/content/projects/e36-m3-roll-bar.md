The car came in stripped for a club road-race season. The owner wanted a six-point bar that
would pass tech without moving the factory seating position more than it had to.

## What we did

- Mocked the bar in place on the car, not on a jig, so it followed the actual shell instead of a
  generic diagram.
- Notched and coped every joint before welding — no filled gaps.
- Fabricated seat mounts for a pair of fixed-back seats and a harness bar sized to the car's
  factory shoulder-belt height.
- Powder coated after fit-check, then final-installed.
