This customer was fighting issues with their car cutting out on track, and the parts cannon hadn't found a resolution yet. Given that the car is 20+ years old, they decided to ditch all of the original wiring in the car. To do so, we determined that the Haltech R3 would be the best solution - being both and ECU and a PDM all in one. Not only does a PDM make configuration and diagnostics much easier, it also makes the wiring cleaner. No more relays and fuses.
